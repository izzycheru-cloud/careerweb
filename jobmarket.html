JOB
MARKET

FPGA and ASIC engineers work in the semiconductor, aerospace, telecommunications, automotive, computing, and consumer-electronics industries. Companies developing CPUs, GPUs, networking hardware, AI accelerators, embedded systems, and other digital hardware need engineers who can design and verify hardware. The field is closely connected to the broader semiconductor industry. Current ASIC/RTL job postings commonly request degrees in Electrical Engineering or Computer Engineering, along with SystemVerilog/Verilog, RTL design, synthesis, timing analysis, and verification skills.
